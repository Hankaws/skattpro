## 4. TOP 5 SKATTPRO OPPORTUNITIES

### #1: FREE TIER FOR LOW-VOLUME FREELANCERS
**Gap:** Zero competitors offer free tier  
**Opportunity:** Free for ≤10 invoices/month or ≤50k NOK/year revenue  
**Conversions:** Upgrade path as freelancers grow  
**Market:** Students, side-hustlers, new ENK owners

### #2: REAL-TIME TAX ESTIMATES
**Gap:** Competitors only do annual filing (extra cost)  
**Opportunity:** Live tax calculation on every invoice  
**Features:**
- "Set aside X%" auto-recommendations
- Quarterly tax projections
- Deduction reminders before deadlines  
**Value:** "Never get surprised at tax time"

### #3: ALL-INCLUSIVE PRICING
**Gap:** Fiken charges extra for bank (59 kr), tax (1,290 kr/year)  
**SkattPro Pro (149 kr/month) includes:**
- ✅ Bank integration
- ✅ Unlimited receipt scanning
- ✅ Quarterly VAT filing
- ✅ Annual tax return
- ✅ Invoice + contract templates  
**Savings:** ~1,500 kr/year vs. Fiken

### #4: FREELANCER-SPECIFIC WORKFLOW
**Gap:** Generic accounting tools, not ENK-focused  
**SkattPro features:**
- Hourly rate calculator (with tax)
- Client payment tracking (Vipps integration)
- Project-based income tracking
- Common freelancer deduction categories
- Contract/proposal generator  
**Value:** "Built for Norwegian freelancers, not businesses"

### #5: 5-MINUTE ONBOARDING
**Gap:** Competitors require manual setup  
**SkattPro:**
- Personnummer integration (Altinn)
- Auto-import from Fiken/Tripletex
- Pre-filled deduction templates
- Interactive first-time ENK tutorial  
**Value:** "From signup to first invoice in 5 minutes"
